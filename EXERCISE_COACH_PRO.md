# Norou Exercise Coach Pro

The exercise coach now includes a local animated SVG body model with:
- front, side and angled viewing states
- movement-direction arrows
- highlighted primary muscle regions
- animated movement cycles for each exercise
- exercise-specific phases and cues
- browser speech synthesis for spoken coaching
- reduced-motion support

The model is deliberately illustrative rather than a medical or biomechanical measurement tool. Form cues are general educational guidance; users should use appropriate resistance and seek qualified professional instruction when needed.
