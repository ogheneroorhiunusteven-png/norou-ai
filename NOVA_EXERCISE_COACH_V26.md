# Nova Exercise Coach v26 — Motion Lab

This update replaces the previous static-reference animation with an interactive, phase-based educational motion system for all 12 Exercise Coach movements.

## What changed
- 12 exercise-specific movement phase sequences.
- Joint/segment transforms for each phase instead of a floating static image.
- Front / Angle / Side viewing modes.
- Target-muscle highlighting tied to the exercise target list.
- Exercise-specific equipment rendering for barbells, dumbbells, cable, and pull-up bar.
- Phase labels and coaching cues update as the motion progresses.
- Slower 0.5× / 0.75× / 1× playback options for technique study.
- Motion and target overlays can be toggled.
- Step-by-step How to mode can pause on any phase.
- Safety Gate language explicitly states that the animation cannot inspect the user's body, load, pain, balance, or equipment setup and cannot guarantee injury prevention.
- Reduced-motion support remains enabled.

## Safety design
The coach is an educational visual guide, not a substitute for in-person instruction or medical assessment. The app deliberately avoids claiming that following the animation guarantees injury prevention. Technique is taught with controlled movement, appropriate range, progressive loading, and stopping when form breaks down or pain occurs.

## Evidence basis
The coaching approach is informed by current resistance-training guidance emphasizing technique, controlled movement, progressive progression, supervision when appropriate, and individualized exercise selection. See the ACSM and NSCA references included with the project update.
